## Added
- Two interactive RCM basics lessons.
- Two medical RCM starter lessons.
- Two dental RCM starter lessons.
- Behavioral health, mental health, DME and vision care starter lessons.
- Browser-only practice sandbox with account, ledger, AR aging and validation views.

- Six Sigma in Healthcare lesson with interactive DMAIC stages and a decision check.
- Interactive visual global healthcare scope roadmap with region cards and phased scope.
- Homepage learning-room gallery and direct master-plan links.

## Scope and safety
- Specialty pages are introductory learning previews, not comprehensive curricula or billing advice.
- International regions are roadmap/future research only; US healthcare RCM is the current foundation.
- Sandbox contains synthetic examples and does not connect to live payer systems. Do not enter PHI/PII.
- KPI formulas are illustrative and simplified. Definitions must be standardized before operational use or benchmarking.
- Regulatory and payer requirements must be checked against current authoritative sources.
