Executive Vision

Project Atlas is a trusted, cloud-ready knowledge and learning ecosystem for Healthcare Revenue Cycle Management. Its purpose is to transform complex healthcare revenue cycle knowledge into something people can learn, understand, practice, apply, measure, and use.

Atlas begins as a rigorous education platform for people entering or advancing in Healthcare RCM — and will grow into an ecosystem connecting learners, RCM professionals, educators, healthcare organizations, employers, and patients seeking to understand their own medical bills.

Mission

Project Atlas exists to make Healthcare Revenue Cycle Management knowledge understandable, practical, trustworthy, and actionable — helping learners build real-world skills and careers, helping organizations develop capable RCM professionals, and ultimately helping patients better understand and advocate for themselves within the healthcare billing system.

North Star

Project Atlas exists to turn Healthcare Revenue Cycle Management knowledge into practical, measurable capability — and ultimately use that knowledge to empower professionals, organizations, and patients.

What Atlas Is Not

  • A static RCM encyclopedia
  • A certification-only website
  • A generic video-course marketplace
  • A chatbot without a trustworthy knowledge foundation
