Why we're building this

From knowing an answer to understanding a system.

Many resources explain codes, rules, and workflows. Our goal is to connect those pieces: the clinical story, the documentation, the claim, the payment, and the decisions that affect the result.

01 / FOUNDATION

Understand the why

Learn the terminology, clinical context, rules, and basic maths that make RCM decisions understandable.

02 / REASONING

Think through the case

Work through fictional scenarios, weigh possible actions, and learn why one next step is stronger than another.

03 / APPLICATION

Practise the work

Build toward guided simulations where learners can follow accounts, claims, balances, and outcomes safely.
