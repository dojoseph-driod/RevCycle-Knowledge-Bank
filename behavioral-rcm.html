Behavioral Health RCM: the evidence-first workflow

Start with the documented service and the rules that apply to it. Confirm provider eligibility, benefit/authorization requirements where applicable, time or unit reporting, documentation, and claim details. Do not assume every behavioral service follows the same rules.

UnderstandIdentify the service and setting.
VerifyCheck the requirements that apply.
DocumentMake the evidence trail visible.
ResolveChoose an action supported by facts.

A claim for a behavioral health service is missing information needed to support the billed service. What is the best response?

Not supported.Do not assume the pathway or requirements without checking the service facts.
Correct reasoning.Start with the record, authoritative guidance and the payer-specific requirements that apply.
Avoid shortcut decisions.The route should reflect the service and rules—not convenience.

Lesson 2

Units, time and service setting.

When a service is reported by time or units, the record, code descriptor, provider qualifications, setting and payer rules must align. Never infer billable units from a schedule alone.

RecordDocument the service delivered.
MatchCompare time/units with applicable coding rules.
CheckReview provider, setting and authorization rules.
SubmitBill only what is supported.

A time-based service has a mismatch between documented time and units on the draft claim. What next?

Not supported.Software edits do not replace documentation or coding requirements.
Correct reasoning.Resolve the discrepancy from evidence and applicable rules.
Never alter a record just to support a claim.Follow approved correction and clarification processes.

Reference starting point: Official coverage / guidance resource. Use the current source and confirm jurisdiction, plan and service details before applying it operationally.
