One focused lesson

Durable Medical Equipment RCM: the evidence-first workflow

Build a pre-bill evidence check: is the item described correctly, is the order/documentation present when required, are coding and modifiers supported, and are supplier/coverage conditions met? Avoid assuming a single checklist applies to every item.

UnderstandIdentify the service and setting.
VerifyCheck the requirements that apply.
DocumentMake the evidence trail visible.
ResolveChoose an action supported by facts.

A required order or supporting record is not available in the account. What should happen?

Not supported.Do not assume the pathway or requirements without checking the service facts.
Correct reasoning.Start with the record, authoritative guidance and the payer-specific requirements that apply.
Avoid shortcut decisions.The route should reflect the service and rules—not convenience.

Lesson 2

Separate delivery, coverage and payment.

Proof of delivery, supplier requirements, item coding, medical documentation and coverage criteria may all play different roles. A single document rarely answers every question.

ItemIdentify the equipment and applicable code.
Order & needConfirm required records are present.
DeliveryCheck evidence and date when required.
CoverageApply the specific policy and payer rules.

Proof of delivery is present. Does that alone prove every coverage requirement is met?

No.Delivery evidence is only one element where applicable.
Correct.Validate the full set of requirements for the specific item and payer.
Incorrect.Follow record-retention and documentation requirements.

Reference starting point: Official coverage / guidance resource. Use the current source and confirm jurisdiction, plan and service details before applying it operationally.
