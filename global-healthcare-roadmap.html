Long-term vision

What “global” must mean

Not copy-and-paste

US billing guidance cannot simply be reused in another country. Coverage, coding, reimbursement, privacy and professional rules differ.

Source before publication

Every regional learning object should state jurisdiction, issuing authority, publication/effective date, last review and intended audience.

The map and roadmap communicate ambition and research sequencing only. They do not mean the Bank currently offers verified curricula in all countries or regions. No dates are promised for future phases.
