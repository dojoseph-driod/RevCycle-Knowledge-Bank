Choose the validation lens

Does the formula match the question?

Confirm numerator, denominator, date range, population, exclusions and treatment of reversals. A percentage without its definition can mislead.

DefineWhat are we measuring?
ScopeWhich claims / dates?
CalculateApply one agreed formula.
ExplainState caveats and use.

Is the underlying data trustworthy?

Check duplicates, missing dates, invalid status values, inconsistent payer groupings, unmatched remittances, and whether the same cohort is used throughout.

Can the team act on the signal?

Assign an owner, a threshold, an escalation path and a review cadence. A dashboard without a defined action can become decoration.

Metric cards worth defining

📥
GCR

Gross collection ratio

Collections ÷ gross charges for the defined cohort; define timing and adjustments.

💰
NCR

Net collection ratio

Collections ÷ collectible net revenue; define contractual adjustments and exclusions.

✅
CCR

Clean claim rate

Claims accepted without a defined preventable error on first submission; define “clean”.

🎯
FPR

First-pass resolution

Share resolved without avoidable rework under a clearly stated definition.

⏱️
AR days

Days in accounts receivable

A proxy for how long revenue remains outstanding; formula and period matter.

🔎
Denial rate

Denied claims / charges

Choose claim-count or dollar basis and use a consistent adjudication cohort.

This page uses simplified educational examples, not a benchmark recommendation. Do not compare organizations until definitions, time windows, populations and accounting treatments are aligned. For Medicare billing context, start with CMS Coding & Billing resources.
