Mission debrief

What you practised

You separated a verification step from a payment guarantee. That distinction matters because eligibility, benefits, authorization, claim edits, medical necessity, and adjudication are related but not interchangeable.

The thinking pattern: establish the facts → understand what the response does and does not say → take the next action supported by the evidence.
